# Military Fleet Art

Two new transparent rasters generated with the built-in image generation tool, using `../v2/contact-sheet.png` as a style reference. Existing sprites are retained unchanged. Palette: blue riveted steel, wood decks, red hull accents, dark pixel outlines.

- `carrier.png`: overhead carrier with empty lower-middle deck cells for two independently rendered aircraft.
- `utility-hull.png`: overhead unarmed hull, used for military battleships, cruisers, support destroyers and decoys. Functional modules are composited onto their exact damage cells by `navalMilitaryArt.js` using existing gun, AA, radar and aircraft art.

Final carrier prompt: One isolated transparent top-down carrier, bow up, 1:2 silhouette, blue riveted steel hull, wood flight deck, small right-side island, white runway dashes, empty lower-middle two deck cells, no planes, no sea, no wake, no text, crisp retro pixel style matching reference.

Final utility hull prompt: One isolated transparent top-down unarmed utility destroyer, bow up, narrow 1:2 silhouette, blue riveted steel hull, wood deck, central cream-window wheelhouse, open front/back deck for overlays, no guns, radar or aircraft, no sea, no wake, no text, matching reference pixel style.

Renderer: `await loadMilitaryArt()` once before drawing. `drawMilitaryShip(ctx, ship, cellSize, {time, aircraftInFlight})` draws local at `(0,0)` within the rotated footprint. Ships use authoritative `kind`, `rotation`, `hits`, `lostPlanes`. `aircraftInFlight` accepts `{shipId, plane}` to hide only the launching aircraft. Hit functional cells have scorched equipment, red damage marks, animated flame and drifting pixel smoke. Lost aircraft leave an empty marked bay. `drawMilitaryAircraft` and `drawMilitaryImpact` support flight and component destruction animation.
