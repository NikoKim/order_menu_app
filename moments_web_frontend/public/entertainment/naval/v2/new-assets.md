# Additional Original Naval Assets

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. Source images are retained under
`E:/Codex/Home/generated_images/01a18b9e-e6c1-7133-917d-9eefc9bb96c8/`.
Delivery PNGs have deterministic alpha cleanup and nearest-neighbor downsampling,
not code-generated artwork. The Phaser scene supplies motion, wake, launch and
impact effects, using the existing authored torpedo and explosion sprites.

- `submarine.png`: top-down elongated three-cell submarine, midnight teal steel,
  mint highlights, brass portholes, twin torpedo ports, red periscope light,
  rivets and panels, aft propeller; refined 16-bit pixel-cartoon naval style,
  upward nose, centered full silhouette, transparent background, no text.
- `stealth.png`: steel canister of iridescent jade-violet stealth paint with a
  mint-tipped brush, tiny ghosted ship emblem, readable 16-bit inventory sprite,
  transparent background, no text or border.

Original prompts requested crisp deliberate pixel clusters, no blur or
antialiasing, generous containment and no scenery or watermark. Raw sources:
`exec-5625927e-5218-4edb-9903-b69119a86685.png` and
`exec-42f719d4-688d-4c99-b095-71a1ad873136.png` respectively.

`audio/submarine.wav` is a dedicated offline-authored stereo sample: low propeller
motor, filtered underwater texture, resonant dive tone and paired launch water.
Rebuild with `node scripts/build-naval-audio.mjs` from the frontend directory.
